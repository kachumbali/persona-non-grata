## Needs a decision

These are not changed. Each needs an owner decision.

1. **Anachronistic vocabulary.** "Hashtag" (press / hashtag `a` text and choice 2 label "#MinisterialVibes", `b` choice 0 "#BellaluxeListens", the hashtag labels and text throughout), "selfie" (press / selfie `a` text), and "goes viral" (press / selfie `a` choice 1 result) and "It trends in Ombrazia" (press / hashtag `a` choice 2 result) are post-2006 vocabulary. The writing guide asks for vocabulary that fits the era, and Team Europe is the only stated anachronism. Decide whether to keep these as a deliberate anachronism or change the words, which would touch several nodes.
2. **Visiting Minister's pronoun.** development / wells `b` choice 1: "Then he'll be photograph 48." This is the only use of a pronoun for the visiting Minister in this file, and it uses "he". CAST open contradiction 1 is unresolved (email:e10 says "her", email:arrival-programme says "his"). Decide the pronoun before this line and the email text are checked together.
3. **People and roles not in the bible.** pavel / stapler `a` choice 5: "Say the Commissioner once lost a stapler too". protocol / seating `a` text: "your Minister is next to the Minister of Fisheries"; choice 1 label: "We demand the President's left hand." None of "the Commissioner", "the Minister of Fisheries" or "the President" is in `docs/CAST.md` or `docs/PLACES.md`. Either add them to the bible or change the lines to refer to roles the bible already has.
4. **Effect against the character's want.** anselm / ghostwriter `a` choice 2, "Brilliant. Send it straight to Capital under your name.", gives trust +6 with Anselm. CAST says he wants credit for the visit programme, and the result does not say he is pleased. The text does not contradict the effect, but the direction may be wrong. Effects are out of scope.
5. **Minister's line on foreign words.** ketu / menu `a` text: "no garlic, no gluten, no foreign words on the menu". The joke aims at the Minister's office and its rule, which fits the house style. But the rule reads close to a nationality-based restriction. Confirm the owner is comfortable with it.
6. **Quoted line in `docs/CAST.md`.** The CAST quote for eu / cookies `a` ("…meeting chairs. Please do not bring a calculator again.") no longer matches the file. The CAST quote should be updated, or the file left as it is. CAST was not edited in this package.
7. **Pending dependencies.** Items 2 and 6 depend on other packages (Q-05c for the email text, and the Q-01 bible). They are listed here so they are not lost.
