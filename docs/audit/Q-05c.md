### Minister pronoun decision

The visiting Minister is referred to with pronouns in one place only.

- `emails.json` `e10`: "greet her personally": female, 1.
- `emails.json` `arrival-programme`, option note: "a programme of his own". The pronoun refers to the Minister's aide, not to the Minister, so it is not a Minister pronoun. It was male, 0 for the Minister.
- All other mentions of "the Minister" in the chapter folder (`campaign.json`, `chapter.json`, `conversation-payoffs.json`, `dialogues.json`, `emails.json`, `invitations.json`, `press.json`, `mail-choices.json`) carry no pronoun. The "his" in `chapter.json` ("Deputy Minister ... his excellent goat") refers to Deputy Minister Bako, not the visitor.

Decision: the visiting Minister is **female** ("her", as in `e10`). The `arrival-programme` note was rewritten to "The Minister's office supplies a programme of its own", so no male pronoun for the Minister remains.

## Needs a decision

1. **Parodies of real companies.** `WRITING_GUIDE.md` says "no real companies". "BlockBerry" (`posting-briefing.json`, "Where things arrive"; used across the chapter) is a thin parody of a real handheld brand. "Chirper" (`emails.json` `e9`) is a thin parody of a real microblogging service. "Chercheur" (`dialogues.json`, outside this package) is a parody of a search engine. Options: keep as deliberate satire and say so in the guide, or rename across the chapter. The renaming touches files outside this package. Not changed.
2. **Rank of Bianca Montefiore.** The rank "Colonel" appears in `emails.json` `e6` and `mail-choices.json` (`e6`), but `char:bianca` has role "Defence Attaché" and no rank (CAST open contradiction 4). Text alone cannot settle it. Options: add the rank to the character record in `campaign.json` (out of scope), or keep the rank in the two mails only. Kept as written, as instructed.
3. **Quote marks.** The six files mix straight quotes (`'`) and curly quotes (`’`, `‘`), as does the rest of the chapter. Options: pick one house style and normalise in a separate package. Not changed.
